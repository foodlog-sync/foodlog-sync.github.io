Food Log Sync

A personal tool that takes meals logged in a private Google Sheet and writes them into the owner's Google Health nutrition log, keeping the food diary up to date without manual entry.

What it does

  • Reads new rows from a private Google Sheet food log.
  • Posts each meal (name, time, calories, protein, carbs, fat) to the Google Health nutrition-log API under the owner's own Google account.
  • Marks rows as synced so nothing posts twice.

What it doesn't do

  • Single user, no sign-ups, no data sharing.
  • It touches only the nutrition log — never workouts, sleep, or any other health data.
