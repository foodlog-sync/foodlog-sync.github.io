Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Food Log Sync is a personal tool with a single user: its author. This policy is short because the data practices are simple.

What data is accessed

With the owner's explicit consent, the tool accesses:

  • Google Sheets: a private food-log spreadsheet (meal names, times, calories, protein, carbs, fat).
  • Google Health: write and read access limited to the nutrition-log data type, used to record the meals from the sheet.

How data is used

Meal rows are copied from the sheet into the Google Health nutrition log. That is the entire use. Data is not sold, shared, analyzed for advertising, or sent to any third party.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored in the script's private properties inside the owner's own Google account. No separate server or database exists.
